Scope & calculation assumptions

Each row is an independent scenario with the full capital input, not a portfolio allocation. Only the simulator's explicitly mapped USDC spot / perp pairs are included; markets without a mapped spot leg are excluded. Missing midpoint prices and failed calculations are shown separately. Pair warnings remain visible, including unverified ticker matches and wrapper / redemption risk.

The same sizing, four taker fees, signed funding replay and hypothetical exit basis calculations as Arbitrage Simulator are used. Half the capital is reserved for spot and half for 1× perp margin. APR simply annualizes modeled net return, including one-time fees and basis P&L; it is not a forecast or realized return. Spread, slippage, borrowing and liquidation are excluded.

All rows share one quote collection and one funding window ending at the latest hourly boundary. Details reuse the saved snapshot and parameters. Refresh & Simulate in the detail tab starts a new calculation. Long windows may take longer due to the shared API request budget; completed history chunks are cached.

Calculation details & assumptions

Any mapped crypto or stock spot / perp candidate with both current midpoint prices can be simulated, regardless of volume or order-book depth. Ticker-only matches may represent different assets or units. Historical funding rates are observed; entry uses current midpoints and exit uses a selected hypothetical gap. Neither is an executable fill or a historical trade.

Fees are editable account estimates. Half the capital is reserved for spot and half for 1× perp margin. Liquidity, bid/ask spread, slippage, borrowing, leverage, reinvestment, other future price moves and liquidation are excluded.

Exit basis · price assumptions

Each fee = matched quantity × that leg's price × fee rate. Percentage inputs are divided by 100 (e.g. 0.07% = 0.0007). The perp rate is the total rate you enter; include any applicable deployer surcharge in it. No separate surcharge is calculated. Funding is shown separately; spread, slippage, borrowing and liquidation costs are excluded. Displayed amounts are rounded; totals use unrounded values.
